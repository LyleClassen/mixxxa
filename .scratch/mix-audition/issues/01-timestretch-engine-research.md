# Research: key-preserving time-stretch in the webview

Type: research
Status: open

## Question

Which engine gives us dual-track, key-preserving tempo sync in the Electrobun webview? Compare SoundTouch WASM (soundtouchjs / @soundtouchjs/audio-worklet), signalsmith-stretch, and RubberBand WASM on: audio quality at ±8% and ±16% stretch, AudioWorklet integration, CPU cost for two simultaneous streams, licensing (RubberBand is GPL/commercial), and sample-accurate start alignment of two players. Output: markdown summary with a recommendation.
